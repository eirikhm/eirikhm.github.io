Tana Inc

Full-time · 5 yrs 6 mos

Tana is an AI-native workspace that blends notes, tasks, and knowledge into a single, structured graph. Instead of scattering information across documents, everything in Tana is a node that can carry fields, relationships, and context - so your notes become living data you can query, reshape, and build on.

On top of that graph, Tana layers AI agents and "supertags" that turn freeform capture into actionable workflows: meeting notes that summarize themselves, tasks that route to the right place, and personal knowledge that compounds over time. The mission is simple but ambitious - reinvent how humans, teams, and computers work together.
