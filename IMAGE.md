# Image Description

The image is a meme styled after a famous scene from *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas*.

## Caption (top text)

> We were somewhere around Beersel, on the edge of the Pajottenland, when the gueuze began to take hold...

## Visual

Below the text is a photo of two men riding in an open-top convertible through a sunny desert landscape. In the foreground on the right, a man wears a white bucket hat, large yellow-tinted aviator sunglasses, and a patterned floral shirt, with a cigarette holder in his mouth — evoking the Raoul Duke character (Johnny Depp). On the left, a second man with dark hair, a mustache, and dark sunglasses leans back, smiling — evoking Dr. Gonzo. The scene mimics the iconic movie still.

## Notes

The caption parodies the film's famous opening line ("We were somewhere around Barstow, on the edge of the desert, when the drugs began to take hold..."), replacing the locations with Belgian references (Beersel and the Pajottenland) and the drugs with "gueuze," a traditional Belgian lambic beer from that region.
